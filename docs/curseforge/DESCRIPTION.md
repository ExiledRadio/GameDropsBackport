# Game Drops Backport

Minecraft's 26.x game drops, 26.1 to 26.3, on Minecraft 1.12.2. It needs Backport Core on the client and the server.

> 💬 [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/kxQvMDJBTN) for bug reports and questions.

## The golden dandelion and the babies

A dandelion and eight gold nuggets make the golden dandelion. Feed one to a baby animal and it stays a baby until you feed it another. It also makes yellow dye and a suspicious stew of Saturation.

Baby animals and zombies get 26.1's new models and textures. Cats, pigs, cows and chickens come with 26.1's sound variants. Name tags can be crafted from paper and a nugget.

![Baby animals in a paddock](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2256/283/01-gd-babies-jpg.jpg)

The other backports' babies join in. Their babies get 26.1's models when the backport has them, from Village & Pillage's foxes and pandas to Trails & Tales' camels, armadillos and puppies of every wolf variant, and the golden dandelion also keeps Wild Update's tadpoles and Trails & Tales' snifflets small.

## Sulfur and cinnabar

Sulfur and cinnabar come polished, as bricks and chiseled, with stairs, slabs and walls for each. Potent sulfur bubbles under water and lets out nauseating gas, and erupts over magma and lava. Sulfur spikes hang from ceilings and grow from floors, and fall when their support breaks.

The music disc Bounce turns up in mineshaft chests.

![A bathhouse built from sulfur and cinnabar](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2256/284/02-gd-sulfur-blocks-jpg.jpg)

## The sulfur cube

A small cube that hops like a slime and swallows the block it lands on. What it holds changes how it acts: a slime block makes it bouncy, honeycomb sticky, magma hot and TNT explosive. Shear it to get the block back. Big cubes go into a bucket, and small ones grow on slimeballs.

![A sulfur cube in a sulfur cave](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2256/313/08-gd-sulfur-cube-jpg.jpg)

## Sulfur caves

A new cave biome under low land, banded with sulfur and cinnabar, with pools of potent sulfur and spikes. It has its own fog, water and plant colors, and F3 names it. Sulfur springs rise from it to the surface. Cave spiders live there instead of spiders, and sulfur cubes spawn there.

## Poplar and the dappled forest

The dappled forest grows in the plains of cool regions: orange grass, poplars in red, orange and yellow, fallen poplars, red shrubs and shelf mushrooms.

Poplar has every wood shape, signs and boats. Red shrubs spread with bone meal. Shelf mushrooms grow on the sides of blocks and bounce you like a bed.

![The dappled forest](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2256/286/04-gd-dappled-forest-jpg.jpg)

![A poplar cabin with shelf mushrooms and red shrubs](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2256/287/05-gd-poplar-wood-jpg.jpg)

## Wool, concrete, cushions and straw beds

Wool and concrete come as stairs and slabs in all 16 colors. Cushions are made from wool slabs, and you can sit on them.

Three hay bales make four straw beds. Sleep through the night on one without setting your spawn. It is used up after one night.

![Wool and concrete stairs and slabs, cushions and straw beds](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2256/288/06-gd-soft-blocks-jpg.jpg)

## Abandoned camps

Abandoned camps turn up in 18 biomes, each built to suit its biome, with loot of their own. Some camp trees carry bee nests. Their chests hold 26.3's explorer maps, which lead to camps, mineshafts, temples, villages, monuments, mansions, swamp huts and the structures of other backports. Explorer maps copy in a crafting grid.

![An abandoned camp under red poplars](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2256/289/07-gd-abandoned-camp-jpg.jpg)

## Menus and screens

26.3's title screen panorama, menu music and splashes. The pause menu has World Options, with 26.3's Game Rules screen and its search, and a LAN switch that turns sharing off again. Optimize World is on the world's edit screen. Borderless fullscreen is the default, as in 26.x, and F3 has its own GUI scale. Every creative tab is in 26.3's order.

## The rest of 26.x

You also get 26.x's smaller changes to things 1.12.2 already has, such as `/time` with world clocks, bed bounce, riding endermen, ocelots on Peaceful, separate chat and command spam limits, `/swing`, and 26.3's replies to `/advancement` and `/team`.

Every feature can be turned off in Backport Core's settings screen, under Mod Options.

## Not included

*   Online services from 26.x: the friends list, Realms invite codes, chat restrictions and player reporting. 1.12.2 has none of them.
*   The Vulkan graphics option. 1.12.2 runs on OpenGL.
*   Drops after 26.3. They join this mod as they ship.

## Compatibility

**[Village & Pillage Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/villager-backport)**: villagers and the wandering trader trade as in 26.3, with this mod's items. The wandering trader sells golden dandelions, name tags, poplar saplings, logs and shelf mushrooms. Its composter takes the new plants and its stonecutter cuts concrete stairs and slabs. Cartography tables copy explorer maps, and village explorer maps lead to its villages.

**[Update Aquatic Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/update-aquatic-backport)**: thrown tridents break sulfur spikes, and explorer maps lead to warm ocean ruins.

**[Buzzy Bees Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/buzzy-bees-backport)**: sulfur cubes swallow its honeycomb blocks and turn sticky, and camp trees get its bee nests.

**[Nether Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nether-backport)**: sulfur cubes swallow its blocks too, and its babies get 26.1's models.

**[Caves & Cliffs Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/caves-cliffs-backport)**: in its 1.18 worlds, sulfur caves sit at 26.2's depth among its lush caves, dripstone caves and deep dark. Note blocks on its copper play the trumpet, and its biome music includes 26.x's new tracks.

**[Wild Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/wild-update-backport)**: poplar chest boats, wool stairs and slabs dampen vibrations, explorer maps lead to ancient cities, and the golden dandelion keeps tadpoles from growing.

**[Trails & Tales Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trails-tales-backport)**: cherry trees grow in the cherry grove camps, baby camels, snifflets, armadillos and every wolf variant's puppies get 26.1's models, and the golden dandelion keeps snifflets small.

**[Tricky Trials Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trials-backport)**: leaf litter under the poplars, note blocks on its copper play the trumpet, explorer maps lead to trial chambers, and paintings show their names. This mod's menu screens replace its 1.21.11 ones.

**OptiFine**: works, shaders included.

**Fluidlogged API** (optional): the new stairs, slabs and walls, sulfur spikes, and poplar fences, trapdoors, signs and leaves hold water, and potent sulfur bubbles under water kept in a block. Without it they stay dry and everything else works.

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.12.2 with Forge 14.23.5.2847 or newer, and [Backport Core](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/backport-core) 1.4.4 or newer. The CurseForge app installs Backport Core with it. [Fluidlogged API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fluidlogged-api) 3.3.3 or newer is optional, for waterlogged blocks.
