# Game Drops Backport

Minecraft's 26.x game drops, 26.1 to 26.3, on Minecraft 1.12.2. It needs Backport Core on the client and the server.

> 💬 [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/kxQvMDJBTN) for bug reports and questions.

## The golden dandelion and the babies

A dandelion and eight gold nuggets make the golden dandelion. Feed one to a baby animal and it stays a baby until you feed it another. It also makes yellow dye and a suspicious stew of Saturation.

Baby animals and zombies get 26.1's new models and textures. Cats, pigs, cows and chickens come with 26.1's sound variants. Name tags can be crafted from paper and a nugget.

## Sulfur and cinnabar

Sulfur and cinnabar come polished, as bricks and chiseled, with stairs, slabs and walls for each. Potent sulfur bubbles under water and lets out nauseating gas, and erupts over magma and lava. Sulfur spikes hang from ceilings and grow from floors, and fall when their support breaks.

The music disc Bounce turns up in mineshaft chests.

![Sulfur and cinnabar blocks in a build](TODO-sulfur-blocks.jpg)

## The sulfur cube

A small cube that hops like a slime and swallows the block it lands on. What it holds changes how it acts: a slime block makes it bouncy, honeycomb sticky, magma hot and TNT explosive. Shear it to get the block back. Big cubes go into a bucket, and small ones grow on slimeballs.

![Sulfur cubes holding different blocks in a sulfur cave](TODO-sulfur-cube.jpg)

## Sulfur caves

A new cave biome under low land, banded with sulfur and cinnabar, with pools of potent sulfur and spikes. Sulfur springs rise from it to the surface. Cave spiders live there instead of spiders, and sulfur cubes spawn there.

![A sulfur cave with a spring and spikes](TODO-sulfur-caves.jpg)

## Poplar and the dappled forest

The dappled forest grows in the plains of cool regions: orange grass, poplars in red, orange and yellow, fallen poplars, red shrubs and shelf mushrooms.

Poplar has every wood shape, signs and boats. Red shrubs spread with bone meal. Shelf mushrooms grow on the sides of blocks and bounce you like a bed.

## Wool, concrete, cushions and straw beds

Wool and concrete come as stairs and slabs in all 16 colors. Cushions are made from wool slabs, and you can sit on them.

Three hay bales make four straw beds. Sleep through the night on one without setting your spawn. It is used up after one night.

![Wool and concrete stairs and slabs, cushions and a straw bed](TODO-soft-blocks.jpg)

## Abandoned camps

Abandoned camps turn up in 18 biomes, each built to suit its biome, with loot of their own. Their chests hold 26.3's explorer maps, which lead to camps, mineshafts, temples, villages, monuments, mansions, swamp huts and the structures of other backports. Copy one with empty maps in a crafting table.

![An abandoned camp in a taiga](TODO-abandoned-camp.jpg)

## The rest of 26.x

You also get 26.x's smaller changes to things 1.12.2 already has, such as `/time` with world clocks, bed bounce and riding endermen, and 26.3's title screen panorama, menu music, splashes and pause menu with World Options.

Every feature can be turned off in Backport Core's settings screen, under Mod Options.

![Game Drops' page in Backport Core's settings screen](TODO-settings.jpg)

## Not included

*   Online services from 26.x: the friends list, Realms invite codes, chat restrictions and player reporting. 1.12.2 has none of them.
*   The Vulkan graphics option and borderless fullscreen. 1.12.2 runs on OpenGL and its fullscreen is always exclusive.
*   Screens for things 1.12.2 lacks: game rules search and world upgrades.
*   Drops after 26.3. They join this mod as they ship.

## Compatibility

**[Village & Pillage Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/villager-backport)**: the wandering trader sells golden dandelions, name tags, poplar saplings, logs and shelf mushrooms. Its composter takes the new plants and its stonecutter cuts concrete stairs and slabs. Village explorer maps lead to its villages.

**[Update Aquatic Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/update-aquatic-backport)**: thrown tridents break sulfur spikes, and explorer maps lead to warm ocean ruins.

**[Buzzy Bees Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/buzzy-bees-backport)**: sulfur cubes swallow its honeycomb blocks and turn sticky, and the camps' bee trees carry bee nests.

**[Nether Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nether-update-backport)**: sulfur cubes swallow its blocks too, and its baby zombified piglins keep its own models.

**[Caves & Cliffs Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/caves-cliffs-backport)**: in its 1.18 worlds, sulfur caves sit at 26.2's depth among its lush caves, dripstone caves and deep dark. Note blocks on its copper play the trumpet.

**[Wild Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/wild-update-backport)**: poplar chest boats, wool stairs and slabs dampen vibrations, and explorer maps lead to ancient cities.

**[Trails & Tales Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trails-tales-backport)**: cherry trees grow in the cherry grove camps.

**[Tricky Trials Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tricky-trials-backport)**: leaf litter under the poplars, note blocks on its copper play the trumpet, and explorer maps lead to trial chambers. This mod's menu screens replace its 1.21.11 ones.

**OptiFine**: works, shaders included.

**Fluidlogged API** (optional): the new stairs and slabs hold water, and potent sulfur bubbles under water kept in a block. Without it they stay dry and everything else works.

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.12.2 with Forge 14.23.5.2847 or newer, and [Backport Core](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/backport-core) 1.4.4 or newer. The CurseForge app installs Backport Core with it. [Fluidlogged API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fluidlogged-api) 3.3.3 or newer is optional, for waterlogged blocks.
