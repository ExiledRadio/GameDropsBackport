GAME DROPS BACKPORT - SCREENSHOT SCENES
=======================================

Twelve pictures for the mod page. Each one goes with one section of the description.

1. gd1-babies.jpg - The golden dandelion and the babies
-------------------------------------------------------
A fenced paddock on a flowery meadow, seen from its open gate. Inside are babies with their new 26.1 models: a calf next
to its mother, two chicks, a piglet or two, a lamb, a foal, a bunny and a kitten. Golden dandelions grow by the gate,
with one potted on a fence post. Hold a golden dandelion in your hand (HUD on, hand visible).

2. gd2-family-babies.jpg - The other backports' babies
------------------------------------------------------
A small clearing at the edge of a mixed forest. Three or four wolf puppies of different variants (pale, ashen, woods,
striped and so on) play together, one wearing a collar. Beside them is a snifflet with an adult sniffer behind it. If
there's a pond, add a few tadpoles in the shallows at the front.

3. gd3-sulfur-blocks.jpg - Sulfur and cinnabar
----------------------------------------------
A small open-fronted bathhouse built from the new blocks: sulfur brick walls on a polished cinnabar base, chiseled
sulfur pillars with chiseled cinnabar caps, and a band of cinnabar bricks. Arched windows are made of sulfur and
cinnabar brick stairs, with stair benches and slab seats inside. In the middle is a pool of water over potent sulfur,
bubbling and giving off gas, ringed with polished sulfur slabs. Stacks of plain sulfur, cinnabar and potent sulfur sit
by the door, and sulfur walls make a low fence outside.

4. gd4-sulfur-cube.jpg - The sulfur cube
----------------------------------------
Inside a sulfur cave, a group of sulfur cubes of mixed sizes, each holding a different block so their looks contrast:
TNT, a slime block, magma, honeycomb, cinnabar, and one empty. Put the big one in the middle with the small ones in
front. Behind them, sulfur spikes and part of a potent sulfur pool.

5. gd5-sulfur-caves.jpg - Sulfur caves
--------------------------------------
A wide view down a sulfur cave in a real sulfur caves biome (check that F3 names it), so its own fog and colors show.
Bands of sulfur and cinnabar run through the walls. Clusters of sulfur spikes hang from the roof and rise from the
floor. At the far end is a pool of water over potent sulfur, lit by a shaft of daylight or a lava seep. A cave spider
on the wall, or a sulfur cube on the floor, gives the scale.

6. gd6-sulfur-spring.jpg - Sulfur spring
----------------------------------------
A large sulfur spring in a grassy valley: a pool rimmed with sulfur, cinnabar, granite and tuff, with spikes rising
from the water and the rim. Take the shot during an eruption, when the plume rises out of the pool. It erupts about
every 50 seconds, so wait for it.

7. gd7-dappled-forest.jpg - The dappled forest
----------------------------------------------
Low to the ground beside a fallen poplar with shelf mushrooms of both sizes on it, looking up a gentle slope into red,
orange and yellow poplars. The ground is the forest's orange grass, with leaf litter, red shrubs and ferns.

8. gd8-poplar-wood.jpg - Poplar wood
------------------------------------
A woodcutter's cabin at the forest edge, built from every poplar shape: plank walls on stripped log posts, a stair roof
with a slab ridge, a poplar door, trapdoor shutters, a slab porch with a fence and an open gate, and a button and a
pressure plate. Outside are a poplar sign reading "Poplar Grove / Woodcutter", a potted poplar sapling, a woodpile of
logs, wood and stripped wood, shelf mushrooms on the posts, red shrubs, and a poplar boat pulled up on the grass or
moored at a stream.

9. gd9-soft-blocks.jpg - Wool, concrete, cushions and straw beds
----------------------------------------------------------------
A garden terrace or patio:
- a sofa of colored wool stairs, with wool slab arms and cushions of several colors on it;
- a rug of two-color wool and wool slabs;
- a coffee table of white concrete slabs;
- wool slab stools with cushions;
- concrete stairs as steps, and concrete slab planters;
- two straw beds with a bit of hay under a lean-to roof of concrete stairs, at the side.
Use a strong, matching palette so the colors read.

10. gd10-abandoned-camp.jpg - Abandoned camps
---------------------------------------------
An abandoned camp under red poplars at the edge of the dappled forest. On one side, a cold campfire, log seats with
cushions, a trodden path and the camp chest. On the other, a tent of white wool stairs over straw beds, with a
cauldron and a barrel. A camp tree with a bee nest and a couple of bees, if one spawned. Open the camp chest first so
it gives you its explorer map, then hold the map in your off hand (HUD on) so it shows beside the camp.

11. gd11-game-rules.jpg - The Game Rules screen
-----------------------------------------------
In a world with cheats on, open the pause menu, then World Options, then Game Rules. Type something in the search box
(for example "keep") so the list is filtered, and take the screenshot with the matching rule showing. This one is a
plain screen capture, with nothing to build. A nice-looking world behind the menu helps.

12. gd12-settings.jpg - The settings screen
-------------------------------------------
Options, then Mod Options, then Backport Core, then Game Drops: the Game Drops page with its feature list showing. A
plain screen capture, with nothing to build.
